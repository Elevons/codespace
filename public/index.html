clear the choice and filters

orbit · right-drag to pan · wheel to zoom

  

  
Filter the map from the Outliner's strip (Alt-click solos a chip); an eye hides a row in the scene. Double-click a layer's name beside the map to pick it out (the other layers fade grey in place), and again to bring them all back. In search, ↑ ↓ pick a match, Enter chooses it, Esc empties it. In the tree, arrows move and fold, Enter chooses, ShiftEnter or a double-click opens the source, Space flips the eye.
